A question worth considering

What happens to us after we die?

Have you ever asked yourself that question?

Find out below ↓

01 / The beginning

Nothing can make itself.

If you look at a building, you can observe that the building is currently there. But how did it get there? It must have had a builder. The building began at one point so it requires a builder to make it.

In the same way, we know that the universe had a beginning, so it too requires a creator.

So, since there is a creator, that creator has the authority to make rules and hold us accountable to those rules. Therefore, when we die, we will be judged by him based on whether or not we have kept those rules.

02 / A good judge

Why does the Creator have to judge?

The Creator is going to judge because he is good. He will not let sin go unpunished. It is actually a good thing that the Creator punishes sin the same way that it is good when the government punishes lawbreakers.

This brings up a very important question:

Have you kept God's rules?

Here is a quick test:

  • Have you ever lied?
  • Have you ever stolen something?
  • Have you ever been unkind?
  • Have you ever disobeyed your parents?
God will judge us compared to perfection, not compared to other people.

03 / The consequences

Wrongdoing has a consequence.

Since God's standard is perfect and we haven't met it, we are deserving of punishment. That place of punishment is called hell and do NOT want to go there. It is described as a lake of fire that never ends.

This is your current situation: In this illustration, you are on the right, and the punishment of hell is coming toward you from the left.

04 / The problem with trying harder

What’s the solution?

Will improving our life or asking for forgiveness fix our problem? No. Here’s why:

Imagine you robbed a bank and were caught. Even if you beg the judge for forgiveness and promise to never do it again and commit to doing good things, the crime you committed still has consequences.

In the same way, even if we pray and ask God for forgiveness and promise to never do anything wrong again and just do good things, it doesn’t get rid of the punishment we deserve for the bad things we have already done. Justice must be done.

Who’s going to pay for you?

In the speeding fine example, the only way to get rid of the fine is to either pay the fine yourself or have someone willing to pay the fine for you.

Someone has to pay for the bad you’ve done: either you in hell forever, or someone perfect on your behalf.

In the same way, either we need to take our own punishment in hell forever, or we need someone perfect who is willing to take our hell punishment for us.

The Creator decided to come to this earth in the person of Jesus. He lived perfectly and volunteered to take our hell punishment for us when he died on the cross. Jesus proved himself to be true by coming back to life three days later.

What is different about this picture?

05 / Your response

You now have a choice to make.

He now gives you a choice to make. Either you:

×

Reject the amazing sacrifice that Jesus made on the cross and therefore take your punishment for your sins in hell forever.

✓

Trust that Jesus took your punishment for you and be certain of the hope of heaven, beginning a life of following him.

The invitation is yours

What will you choose today?
