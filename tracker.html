Made for real life

Keep the focus on people, not pressure.

The tracker is being planned as a gentle companion to help you be intentional, remember what matters, and grow over time.

01 / REMEMBER

Keep a simple log

Jot down meaningful moments and conversations while details are fresh—at the level of detail that feels right to you.

02 / REFLECT

Notice what you’re learning

Use thoughtful prompts to reflect on listening, courage, questions, and the ways you’re growing in sharing your faith.

03 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Be intentional with care

Remember to pray, reconnect, or continue a conversation—without turning a person into a task or a tally.

A gentle rhythm

Reflect. Learn. Keep showing up.

Set an intention

Choose a small, personal goal that helps you be present and prepared in everyday life.

Remember the moment

After a conversation, record a private note or reflection to help you remember what mattered.

Grow at your pace

Look back on your journey, celebrate faithfulness, and bring what you’re learning into the next day.

A word about privacy

People are not data points.

Real conversations can include sensitive and personal details. The future app should be designed with care, giving you control over what you choose to record and encouraging discretion about other people’s stories.

This page is only a preview. The tracker is not connected yet, and it does not save or collect entries. Before launch, the app’s privacy and data-handling details should be clearly explained.

Rooted in the Gospel

Go into every conversation with grace.

The tracker is one part of a bigger journey: knowing the good news, loving your neighbor, and sharing hope with gentleness and respect.
